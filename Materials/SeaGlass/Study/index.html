What this material is made of +

Backdrop diffusion, a low-opacity tinted body, static fine grain, soft contact shadow, denser inset edges and a narrow directional rim. The label, hit targets and values are independent of the decorative material. There is no physical refraction solver in this study.

In Metro Station, sea glass is a deliberate satin alternative for a control or isolated lens. Keep supporting panels opaque; retain semantic cyan, amber and violet signals independently of the chosen surface tint. Do not infer a live measurement from the decorative traces.
